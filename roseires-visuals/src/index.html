Why use radar and optical imagery?

NISAR sends microwaves and measures the signal that bounces back, called backscatter. It can observe through clouds and in darkness. Water, wet soil, and changes in surface roughness can all affect the return.

Sentinel‑2 uses reflected sunlight to classify land and water where the scene is clear. Comparing the two helps check whether a change in radar return matches a change in the water map.
